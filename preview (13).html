Who We Are

About ARM Public School

ARM Public School is a learning community built on a simple belief — that education should develop both the mind and the character. Located in Ramgulam Ka Purwa, Mukundpur, Kunda, Pratapgarh, the school provides a supportive environment where students are encouraged to learn with curiosity, act with discipline and grow with confidence.

We believe that academic learning, values, creativity and confidence should grow together. Every child is encouraged to participate, express ideas and develop the skills that will serve them well beyond the classroom.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, responsible and future-ready learners by combining strong academic foundations with values, creativity and holistic development.

Our Mission

To create a positive and supportive learning environment where quality teaching, discipline and encouragement help every student discover and develop their individual potential.

Learning at ARM

Our Academic Approach

We aim to make learning engaging, meaningful and student-focused — so that children understand concepts rather than simply memorising them.

01

Foundation & Primary Learning

Building strong basics in language, numbers and understanding from the early years.

02

Concept-Based Learning

Encouraging clear understanding of concepts instead of rote learning.

03

Activity & Creativity

Learning through activities that develop imagination, expression and curiosity.

04

Communication & Confidence

Helping students express themselves clearly and participate with confidence.

At ARM Public School, our academic approach focuses on making learning engaging, meaningful and student-focused. We encourage curiosity, clear understanding and active participation so that every child develops a strong foundation for future learning.
